About The Project

A Django-based platform designed for online game selling and digital library management.

SellGame Online is a modern Django-based game store platform where users can browse games, add games to cart, manage wishlist items, purchase games, access owned games in their library, and download purchased games. The system also includes admin tools for adding, updating, deleting games, managing prices, discounts, categories, images, purchases, users, and sales records.
