About The Project

A Django-based platform designed for online game selling and management.

SellGame Online is a Django-based game store platform where users can browse games, add games to cart, manage wishlist items, purchase games, access owned games in their library, and download purchased games. The system also includes staff management features such as adding, updating, deleting games, managing prices, discounts, categories, images, purchases, and users.
